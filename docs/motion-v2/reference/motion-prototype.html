Atelier / Brand system 01 / Motion v2

Three pieces, four states

While a build is working, the pieces keep coming apart and trying to fit together in the wrong ways. On a confirmed success they click into the A with a green pulse, then return to their own colours. On a warning they balance into an exclamation mark, and on an error they cross into an X.

Working

The pieces keep trying to fit. There are three wrong fits: Head, Bench and Ramp. Between Head and Ramp the pieces go straight across, one at a time, in about two seconds. To or from Bench they come apart first and swing round, which takes about three. Out of an outcome, all three open together in one brisk 420ms move. Then they dock, tap once to test the fit, and give up. None may look like an A, an exclamation mark or an X; those shapes are reserved for outcomes.

Complete

About a second from the result to rest. All three move at once, 340ms: the tile and disc go to their seats while the mesh stands off. The mesh comes in, already wound up, 110ms, then snaps 80ms (hard ease-in, presses 3u past the seat). On contact the disc squashes 12%, the glyph punches to 104.5%, a ripple leaves the disc and green spreads outwards: disc, then tile +60ms, then mesh +120ms. Recoil 110ms, rest 130ms. When the effects have finished the pieces return to their own colours over 380ms, and the status sentence stays green. At 24px and under the mark stays green. The optional sound is a tick, a thump, then a two-note chime.

Warning

A balancing act. The disc goes down as the dot, the mesh stands up on it, and the tile is set on top, a little off-centre. It turns amber, catches its balance in a few decaying sways with the tile lagging the stem, then wobbles gently for as long as the warning stands.

Error

Tile and mesh squeeze into matching bars and cross at ±42°, with the disc pinned at the crossing. The cross clamps shut and turns rose. No shaking, no particles.

Loaders

Where the real asset type is known, one piece does the work instead of the Working loop. The pieces lie in a row (mesh, tile, disc), and the other two stay still. Mesh: it steps up and snaps side to side as lines of halo gap cut it into a different structure each time. Texture: it steps up and splits into five bands that pop through colour plates out of line, then drop into register and close on its exact blue. Material: the disc rolls along the baseline and back while a flat lighter band sweeps across it. From the row, Complete has the mesh hop over the tile.

Overlap rule

Pieces can pass in front of each other, but every piece carries a background-coloured halo 14u wide. They never visually merge, even when all three are the same colour. The tile and mesh carry their notch and bites in the A and the wrong fits, so the animated A is the brand mark exactly. In the ! and X they flatten into plain bars.

Physical, never faded

Pieces move one at a time along planned curves that keep a 14u gap, measured on the outlines actually drawn. The exceptions are Complete and the opening out of an outcome, where all three move together to keep them brisk. The layer order is fixed (tile at the back, mesh in the middle, disc in front), so nothing ever re-stacks or fades. In the rare move with no clear path, one piece simply passes in front of another.

Where it's allowed

The status glyph, plus one entrance for the header logo on launch, in category colours, with no click. The app icon, lockups and static brand mark always use the category colours and the master A.
