Atelier / Brand system 01 / Motion v1.2

Three pieces, four states

While a build is working, the pieces keep coming apart and trying to fit together in the wrong ways. On a confirmed success they click into the A and turn green. On a warning they balance into an exclamation mark, and on an error they cross into an X.

Working

The pieces keep trying to fit. They come apart, each swings round to the side it will dock from, the anchor settles in the middle, and the others come in one at a time, dock, tap twice to test the fit, and give up. There are six wrong fits: Head, Bench, Swap, Totem, Ramp and Tumble. None may look like an A, an exclamation mark or an X; those shapes are reserved for outcomes.

Complete

The tile settles, the mesh comes in from the right and the disc rises from below, all stopping just short. Then wind-up 120ms, (draw back 10u), snap 80ms (hard ease-in, presses 3u past the seat). On contact the disc squashes 12%, the glyph punches to 104.5%, a ripple leaves the disc and green spreads outwards: disc, then tile +60ms, then mesh +120ms. Recoil 110ms, rest 130ms. The optional sound is a tick, a thump, then a two-note chime.

Warning

A balancing act. The disc goes down as the dot, the mesh stands up on it, and the tile is set on top, a little off-centre. It turns amber, catches its balance in a few decaying sways with the tile lagging the stem, then wobbles gently for as long as the warning stands.

Error

Tile and mesh squeeze into matching bars and cross at ±42°, with the disc pinned at the crossing. The cross clamps with a 3° overshoot and turns rose. No shaking, no particles.

Overlap rule

Pieces can pass in front of each other, but every piece carries a background-coloured halo 14u wide. They never visually merge, even when all three are the same colour. In the A this halo is what cuts the bites.

Physical, never faded

Pieces move one at a time along planned curves that keep a 14u gap from everything else. The layer order is fixed (tile at the back, mesh in the middle, disc in front), so nothing ever re-stacks or fades. In the rare move with no clear path, one piece simply passes in front of another.

Where it's allowed

The status glyph only. The app icon, lockups and static brand mark always use the category colours and the master A.
