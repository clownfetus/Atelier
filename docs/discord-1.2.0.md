**Atelier 1.2.0**

Atelier's own mark now shows its work, and pop-ups open and close more calmly.

• The assembly glyph replaces the coloured ink and Krackle: a blue texture tile, a teal mesh block and a copper material disc try fits while a job runs, then click into the A only when it has actually succeeded.
• Warnings and errors balance into a ! or close into an X, beside a sentence that says what happened.
• Loaders for each kind of asset: models, textures and materials each have their own, and the model stays in view while its textures load.
• Menus, tooltips, toasts and dialogs open out of their own edge and fold back in. Nothing fades.
• Mod tray rows click into place, and the texture, mesh and material studios wear their piece's colour.
• Only textures, meshes and materials keep a category colour now; every other category is neutral and told apart by its icon.
• Full, Calm and Off motion settings, plus optional Motion sounds. Try it in Settings → Preview motion.
• Send to Blender (live) works again on Blender 4.4 and newer, including 5.1. Reinstall Atelier Live Bridge once from Set up live Blender; Atelier tells you if Blender has an older copy.
• On Windows, a pipette beside every colour swatch picks a colour from anywhere on screen and applies it straight away.

Windows installer, portable ZIP and Linux AppImage, with source, screenshots and checksums.

Download: https://github.com/clownfetus/Atelier/releases/tag/1.2.0

Choose Download Update inside Atelier, or install over the existing location. Showcase images now use a plain neutral backdrop. Projects, mods and exports are unchanged.
